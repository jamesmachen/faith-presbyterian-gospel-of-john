# The Gospel of John — Student Study Guide

## Goal

What makes the Gospel of John good news for you and for the world? If it is good news, how can you share it with those God has put in your life?

## Before class

1. Read the assigned passage in more than one translation.
2. Write the passage in your own words.
3. Mark the key word or phrase and explain why it matters.

## Questions for study

1. What is the key word or phrase in the passage? Why did you select it?
2. How does the passage expand your knowledge of Jesus as your Savior? What does it reveal about you?
3. Why is this passage part of the good news John proclaimed? Does it challenge the world’s wisdom?
4. How does the passage reflect the Old and New Testament story?
5. How could you use this passage to tell someone about the Gospel’s impact in your life?

## A Gospel challenge

- What is the essence of the Gospel message?
- What is the most unbelievable thing about the Gospel?
- What can one expect from a life following Jesus?
- How would you tell your story to a friend?
